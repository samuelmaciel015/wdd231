Basic AVSEC Course

The Basic AVSEC Course is the initial course that introduces a professional to the area of ​​Civil Aviation Security, enabling them for various AVSEC-related activities such as:

- Supervising passenger transit between the boarding area and the aircraft;
- Identification and access control of people and objects to restricted or controlled areas of airports;
- Identification and access control of people to the aircraft;
- Identification and inspection of people and vehicles in vehicle access control;
- Inspection of Onboard Provisions or in-flight service;
- Inspection of Sterile Area;
- Inspection of baggage, cargo and objects in general using explosive detection dogs;
- Inspection of people;
- Aircraft security inspection;
- Manual inspection of objects;
- Patrolling and surveillance of in-flight service production and storage facilities and terminals. Cargo or mail;
- Patrolling and surveillance of the operational area of ​​airports;
- Patrolling and surveillance of access control channels;
- Performing surveillance of onboard provisions, onboard service, cargo or mail, or checked baggage;
- Consolidation of the flight's AVSEC dispatch;
- Protection of sterile areas or aircraft;
- Receiving provisions on the aircraft;
- Receiving and identifying cargo or mail in the secure cargo chain; and
- Aircraft security verification.

It's a vast area of ​​opportunities that you will have to develop professionally.

Security Inspection Course

The Security Inspection Course is a course that specializes a professional in the area of ​​Civil Aviation Security, in addition to the activities enabled by the Basic AVSEC course. Some of these activities are:

- Inspection of baggage, cargo and objects in general by means of X-rays or tomographs;
- Inspection of people by means of body-scan;
- Search (personal search) of people;
- Supervising and monitoring the inspection and search of passengers and baggage;
- Supervising and evaluating the on-the-job training for security inspection certification; and
- Conducting quality control tests.

This course further increases the range of opportunities you will have in the AVSEC area by specializing in the activities listed above, being a great differentiator.

AVSEC Passenger Service Course

The AVSEC Passenger Service Course aims to qualify professionals to perform all passenger service for Airline Operators in both commercial and executive aviation with ANAC certification. Some of these activities are listed below:

- Passenger service at check-in (passenger dispatch) or identification and acceptance (reconciliation) of checked baggage;
- Supervising passenger transit between the boarding area and the aircraft;
- Identification and access control of people to the aircraft; and
- Protection of sterile area or aircraft.
